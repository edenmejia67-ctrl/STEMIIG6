# Paper Fix Tracker
### Live Team Checklist for Our Research Paper

This website helps our group organize, assign, and track corrections for **Chapter 1 and Chapter 2** of our research paper. Each task identifies what needs to be fixed, where it appears, the suggested correction, and why it matters.

## How to Use the Tracker

1. **Open the website** using the link above. You can use your phone, laptop, or tablet.
2. **Select your initials** from the “I am” dropdown at the top:
   - EL
   - JJ
   - JP
   - KT
   - MT
3. **Find your assigned tasks.** Use the initials filter to display your tasks and the team's shared decisions.
4. **Read the correction details.** Each task includes:
   - **Find:** The existing text or problem to locate.
   - **Change:** The proposed correction or action.
   - **Why:** The reason the correction is necessary.
5. **Tick the checkbox** after completing a correction.
6. **Add a note** if you need to explain your progress, mention a page number, or report an issue.
7. **Check the progress bar** to see how many tasks have been completed.

## Team Rules

- Complete tasks in the order shown whenever possible. Earlier decisions affect later sections.
- Coordinate with the group before changing shared decisions, especially the research variables, observation period, and statistical treatment.
- Read the **Find**, **Change**, and **Why** fields before editing the research paper.
- Tick a task only after the correction has actually been made and checked.
- Use notes to communicate blockers or explain incomplete work.
- Review the updated document before marking a task complete.
- Do not assume a correction is final if it conflicts with the agreed research design.
- Keep the master research document organized and avoid overwriting another member's edits.

## Live Sync and Troubleshooting

The checklist is designed to share task status across devices through Firebase.

- Keep an internet connection while using the tracker.
- Select your initials before ticking a task.
- Wait briefly after making a change to allow it to sync.
- If your changes do not appear on another device, reload the website and check again.
- If the page reports a Firebase or live-sync error, inform the group member managing the tracker.
- If the tracker says it is saving only on your device, your changes may not be shared with the group yet.

**Important:** Do not assume a change was shared simply because the checkbox changed on your screen.

## Privacy and Access

The tracker is intended for coordinating research-paper corrections. Do not enter passwords, private information, or sensitive personal data into task notes. Anyone with access to the public tracker may be able to view or modify shared task data.

## Our Goal

Finish corrections systematically, resolve research-design inconsistencies as a team, and ensure the final paper is consistent across the Statement of the Problem, Scope, Conceptual Framework, research instruments, data-gathering procedures, and statistical treatment.
